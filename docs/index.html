The Risk

Damage is a probability, not a certainty. The deposit eats it first — you only carry what spills over.

Assistance animals are not pets — no pet rent, fee or deposit applies to them, and many jurisdictions cap pet deposits or ban pet fees outright. Check your lease law before you charge a dime. This is general information, not legal advice.
